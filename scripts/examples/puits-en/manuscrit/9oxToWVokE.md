“Is anyone there?”

Your voice goes down, bounces, and then another voice comes back up. It isn’t yours.

It is a child’s voice. A little hoarse, a little teasing, with a lisp you had forgotten and recognise from the very first word.

“You took ages.”

Your knees hit the stone. You don’t know when your legs gave way. Your hands grip the rim, your face hangs over the void.

“I counted,” the voice goes on. “I counted every single day. On the lid. Granny used to sit on it at night, so I couldn’t get out. But she doesn’t sit there any more.”

A laugh, down below. Bright, delighted.

“Tell me your name. So I’m sure it’s you.”

Something deep in your chest goes stiff. A very old caution, one that doesn’t come from you.
