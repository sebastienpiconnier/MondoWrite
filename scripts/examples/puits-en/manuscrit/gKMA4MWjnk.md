Your foot comes down on the twenty-third rung.

It gives way.

The rotten iron snaps like a dry branch, and your body tips backwards into the void, all your weight at once. Time stretches. You have time to see the piece of iron spin beneath you, to hear it fall, for a long time, far too long, before the splash.

Higher up, you think you hear someone stifle a cry. Or a laugh.
