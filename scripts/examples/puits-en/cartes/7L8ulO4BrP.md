title: "The root corridor"
---
The passage between the green door at the bottom of the well and [@The farm below](cosmos:WNh9vyU3T6). Low ceiling, walls made of the roots of the walnut tree in the yard, a bitter smell of walnut husks. The roots whisper the names of the children who went down, then the reader’s, and wait for an answer. At the end, a second green door identical to the first. A threshold between the two worlds: corridor, mirror or years.
