The reader never told anyone: on the night of 14 August 2003 they were awake, at the window, and saw their brother go to the well. They chose not to move, then to forget. Revealed in The Upside-Down Well; confessed to [@Paul](cosmos:8290ZuJdRK) in The Confession. Only Paul and [@Ourdas](cosmos:nKFYoFi_Cw) knew (“You breathe too loud”).
