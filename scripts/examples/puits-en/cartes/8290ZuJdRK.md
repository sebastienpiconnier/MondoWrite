The little brother. Vanished into the well on the night of 14 August 2003, aged seven. Down there time does not pass: he is still seven, striped pyjamas, scraped knees, a cowlick, a lisp.

Counts everything (stairs, rungs, seconds): “once you have counted something, it can’t scare you any more.” Signs with a P, often backwards.

His treasure: the Dragon’s eye marble. Calls his older sibling “the other one” when he is cross.

Does not recognise the grown-up reader, except through the marble or the confession (word RECOGNISED).
