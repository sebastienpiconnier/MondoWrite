title: "The black lake"
---
At the end of the field of grey wheat in [@The farm below](cosmos:WNh9vyU3T6). The same water as at the bottom of the well, seen from the other side: black, smooth, immense. Pebbles sink into it without a sound or a ripple. [@Paul](cosmos:8290ZuJdRK) sits at its edge. It is from this lake that [@Ourdas](cosmos:nKFYoFi_Cw) rises.
