title: "The rope"
---
A hemp rope coiled on the big nail in the workshop, fifteen metres, as thick as a thumb. One end cut clean, recently. The reader ties it to the pulley beam with the bowline [@Odile](cosmos:t5FPOkVhie) taught for tethering calves. It saves them at the broken rung.
