title: "Odile"
---
The grandmother. Died in her armchair at 3:12, buried on the day the book begins.

As a child, it was her turn to go down in 1958; her brother Marcel went in her place. Once mistress of the farm, she had the oak lid put on. Fell asleep a single night in forty years: the night Paul went down. Ever since, sat on the lid every night so that Ourdas could not call the other child.

Left a letter and a notebook (Odile’s Letter), with the name OURDAS. Slapped the reader only once, when they tried to open the drawer.
