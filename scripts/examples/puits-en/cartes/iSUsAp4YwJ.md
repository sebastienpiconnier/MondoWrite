A black leather notebook swollen with damp, in the bureau drawer, with [@Odile](cosmos:t5FPOkVhie)’s letter to the reader. Full of drawings of the farm upside down under a sky that ripples like water: Odile saw [@The farm below](cosmos:WNh9vyU3T6), or dreamt it. Last page: OURDAS, in capitals, underlined three times.

The letter tells of the pact, of 1958, of the night of 2003, and gives the instruction: “never give it yours”.
