title: "The night of 14 August 2003"
---
A hot night, crickets, a full moon. [@Odile](cosmos:t5FPOkVhie) falls asleep in her armchair, the only time in forty years. [@Paul](cosmos:8290ZuJdRK), seven, crosses the yard in striped pyjamas, sandals in hand, head tilted as if listening to a song, and goes down. From the bedroom window, the reader (ten) sees him, hesitates, and goes back to bed, pretending to be asleep.

The gendarmes drag the water for three days. Nothing. Odile circles the 14th in red on the calendar and marks every anniversary for twenty-three years.
