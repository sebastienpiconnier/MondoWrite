title: "The lantern"
---
A paraffin lantern waiting on the workbench, already filled, matches beside it. Someone (a child?) has tried to reach it: small handprints in the dust. It can light itself if the reader sets off in the dark.

Its flame (0 to 3) is the game’s gauge: flattened by the wind in the well, damaged in the fall, worn down by the marks, refilled in the barn below. Without it, [@Ourdas](cosmos:nKFYoFi_Cw)’s name is not enough. In The Morning, it answers the name and turns white and immense.
