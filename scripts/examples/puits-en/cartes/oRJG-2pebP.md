title: "Marcel goes down (1958)"
---
[@Odile](cosmos:t5FPOkVhie), nine, hears the voice: it is her turn. She is shaking so much she can’t get her clogs on. Her brother [@Marcel](cosmos:Uu1WZvJKWt) goes in her place: “It’s nothing, I’ll be back.” He doesn’t come back. The source of Odile’s guilt, and an exact mirror of the reader’s relationship with [@Paul](cosmos:8290ZuJdRK).
