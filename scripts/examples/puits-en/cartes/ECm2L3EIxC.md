title: "The 3:12 clock"
---
The kitchen clock, above the stove, stopped at twelve minutes past three. It strikes once at that hour “for no reason, and has for years”. Probably the hour [@Paul](cosmos:8290ZuJdRK) went down in 2003, and the hour of [@Odile](cosmos:t5FPOkVhie)’s death. In The House, something scratches at the reader’s door at 3:12.
