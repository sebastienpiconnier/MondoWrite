title: "The Combes pact"
---
Struck by [@Odile](cosmos:t5FPOkVhie)’s great-grandfather during a great drought: the Combes will never run short of water; in return, in every generation, a child of the house goes down of their own accord.

Children who went down: Étienne 1874, Jeanne 1903, [@Marcel](cosmos:Uu1WZvJKWt) 1958, [@Paul](cosmos:8290ZuJdRK) 2003. Their names are carved by the door at the bottom (The Marks).

Broken if [@Ourdas](cosmos:nKFYoFi_Cw)’s name is spoken in front of him with light: the well dries up.
