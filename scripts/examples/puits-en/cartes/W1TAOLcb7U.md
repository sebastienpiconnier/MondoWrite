title: "Is Paul really Paul?"
---
[@Ourdas](cosmos:nKFYoFi_Cw) speaks with Paul’s voice and has all the memories of the children he keeps. Is the voice in the well Paul, or Ourdas imitating him? The text keeps the doubt alive until the lake: it is the marble or the confession, things Ourdas cannot imitate, that prove the child by the water is the real one.
