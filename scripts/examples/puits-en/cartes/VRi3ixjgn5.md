title: "The buyers"
---
A young couple with two children who are to buy [@The Combes farm](cosmos:5xmLViOKEx). Never seen directly: they exist only through the threat they represent. In The Black Water, their little girl leans over the rim; in The Stolen Voice, the false reader sells them the farm and leaves them the key to the padlock. If the pact is not broken, they are the next generation.
