title: "The price of water"
---
The family’s prosperity, paid for by its children. The Combes never run short of water, even in a drought, because one child in every generation goes down. Breaking the pact means accepting a dry well (ending The Name). A theme of sacrifice handed down the generations, and of who agrees to pay in place of others ([@Marcel](cosmos:Uu1WZvJKWt), the reader in The Exchange).
