The character played by the reader, written as “you”, with no fixed gender. [@Paul](cosmos:8290ZuJdRK)’s older sibling, three years older. Comes back to the Combes farm for [@Odile](cosmos:t5FPOkVhie)’s funeral, twenty-three years after their brother vanished.

**Wound:** on the night of 14 August 2003, saw Paul cross the yard towards the well and pretended to be asleep. Buried the memory (revealed in The Upside-Down Well).

**Arc:** to stop “pretending to be asleep”. The ending “The House” replays the failure; the ending “The Morning” mends it.
