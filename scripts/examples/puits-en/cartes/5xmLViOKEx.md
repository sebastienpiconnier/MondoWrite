title: "The Combes farm"
---
The family farm. Gravel yard, two-hundred-year-old walnut tree, stone trough, leaning barn, house with green shutters, workshop under a lean-to. The well at the far end of the yard, sealed since 2003 with an oak lid banded with iron and a padlock.

In the kitchen: a clock that strikes once at 3:12, a walnut bureau with a locked drawer, a photo of the two children on the rim of the well. A green door with a cat flap and a bike scrape at hip height.

To be sold before the end of the month. It has never run short of water.
