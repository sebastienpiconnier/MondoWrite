Variables in the `choix` blocks:

**Light**

-   `lantern` (true/false) and `flame` (0 to 3): flame = 3 when you take the lantern. −1 climbing down the ladder, −1 if you catch yourself at the broken rung without the rope, −1 examining the marks. +1 refilling the lantern in the barn below.

**Objects**: `rope`, `key` (The Workshop), `notebook` (Odile’s Letter), `marble` (The Niche).

**Words**

-   `ourdas`: Odile’s Letter, The Marks or The Kitchen Below.
-   `twenty_three`: The Silence; lets you step over the broken rung.
-   `memory`: The Upside-Down Well.
-   `recognised`: The Marble or The Confession.
-   `name_given`: saying your name in The Voice; forces The Stolen Voice.

**Endings**: The House, The Black Water, The Stolen Voice, The Exchange, The Name, The Morning (ourdas + recognised + flame > 0).
