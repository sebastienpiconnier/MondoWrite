[@Odile](cosmos:t5FPOkVhie)’s dog: yellow, thin, one broken ear. Vanished the first winter after Paul; Odile said he had gone to look for him. Found again in the barn below, the only animal that still moves. He has spent twenty-three years beside [@Paul](cosmos:8290ZuJdRK) and leads the reader to him.
