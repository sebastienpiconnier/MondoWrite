title: "Pretending to sleep"
---
The central theme: the guilt of not having acted, and chosen forgetting. The reader pretended to be asleep in 2003; the voice reminds them (“You’re breathing like when you pretended”). The ending The House replays the failure exactly. The confession to [@Paul](cosmos:8290ZuJdRK) and the ending The Morning mend it: waking up, at last. An echo in [@Odile](cosmos:t5FPOkVhie), who fell asleep only once and then kept watch all her life.
