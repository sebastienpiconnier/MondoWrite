title: "The well"
---
At the far end of the yard, it “breathes like a sleeping animal”. White rim, pulley on a beam. Forty rusty iron rungs set into the wall, which nobody had ever seen; the twenty-third is broken. The walls go from mossy brick to a black, glistening stone. A warm wind rises from the bottom, in breaths out: the well is breathing, and you climb down its throat.

A smell of wet stone and forgotten apples. At the bottom, a ledge, black water that reflects nothing but stars, and the green door. Around the thirtieth rung, [@The niche](cosmos:ZPv3kYSJv7).
