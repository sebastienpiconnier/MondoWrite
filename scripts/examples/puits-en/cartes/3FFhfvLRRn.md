[@Paul](cosmos:8290ZuJdRK)’s big glass marble, with a red spiral running through it. He never lent it: “I’ll give it to you when you’re grown up.” Found in the niche in the well, among the treasures of the children who went down before (The Niche). Shown to Paul, it makes him recognise the reader.
