title: "The lid and the padlock"
---
An oak lid banded with iron and a padlock the size of a fist, put on by [@Odile](cosmos:t5FPOkVhie) after 2003. Its inner face is scratched with hundreds of small marks: [@Paul](cosmos:8290ZuJdRK) counted the days on it. Odile sat on it every night (“As long as I sit on it, he can’t come up”). On the evening of the story it leans against the rim, padlock open: nobody knows who took it off.
