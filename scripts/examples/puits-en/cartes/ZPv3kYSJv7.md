title: "The niche"
---
A hollow the size of a bread oven in the wall of [@The well](cosmos:ldQZvO2Het), around the thirtieth rung. The children who go down leave their treasure there on their way past: tin soldier, [@Jeanne](cosmos:Cr_QG9CABe)’s ribbon, spinning top, harmonica, porcelain doll’s head, marbles, knucklebones, elder whistle. And, apart, the [@Dragon’s eye](cosmos:3FFhfvLRRn). A small warm hand gently pushes the marble towards the reader.
