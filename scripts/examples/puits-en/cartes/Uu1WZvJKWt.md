[@Odile](cosmos:t5FPOkVhie)’s brother, who went down in her place in 1958, aged nine. Grey wool shorts, side parting, Odile’s grey eyes.

Lives in the kitchen below, where he lays the table (one more plate for the reader, “just in case”). Regrets nothing. Is careful never to ask anyone’s name. Gives the name OURDAS and the rules: you need light, and Paul must recognise the reader.
