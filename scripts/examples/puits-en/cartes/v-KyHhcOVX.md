title: "Counting so you’re not afraid"
---
[@Paul](cosmos:8290ZuJdRK)’s rule: “once you have counted something, it can’t scare you any more.” A motif that runs through the whole book: the stairs (thirteen), the rungs (forty, the twenty-third broken), the days scratched on the lid and on the wall, Odile’s crosses on the calendar. In The Black Water, counting becomes the only thing left. In The Morning, the two brothers count together.
