A Combes child who went down in 1903. Left a blue satin ribbon in the niche, carefully rolled and labelled with her name, and carved a little flower next to her name on the wall at the bottom.

Ourdas often borrows her voice: it is with hers that he announces [@Odile](cosmos:t5FPOkVhie)’s death and accepts the exchange.
