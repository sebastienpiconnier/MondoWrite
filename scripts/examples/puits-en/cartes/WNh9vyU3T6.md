title: "The farm below"
---
The double of [@The Combes farm](cosmos:5xmLViOKEx) beneath the well, reached by the green door and the corridor of walnut roots. The same yard to the centimetre, but silent: instead of a sky, the surface of the well water seen from below, whose reflections make stars. A grey light without shadows, except the reader’s.

The well rises upside down there, up to the sky of water; in it you see the night of 14 August 2003. In the barn, the lost animals and Fanfan the dog. In the kitchen, [@Marcel](cosmos:Uu1WZvJKWt). At the end of a field of grey wheat, the black lake where [@Paul](cosmos:8290ZuJdRK) waits.

You can make only one detour there.
