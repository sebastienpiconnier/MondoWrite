“The one who drinks”. Something very old and very thirsty under the farm: deep down, just a thirst that has learnt to speak.

Takes the form of a column of black water in which the faces of the children he has kept slowly turn. Speaks with their voices, and with the voice of anyone who gives him their name.

**Rules:**

-   Never takes anyone by force: he calls, and they go down.
-   Knowing someone’s name gives him their voice and shape (word NAME GIVEN).
-   Saying his name in front of him forces him to listen and breaks the pact, but only with light.
-   Drinks time as well: down below, children don’t grow older.
